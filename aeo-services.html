What Is Answer Engine Optimization?

Traditional SEO fights for indexed blue hyperlinks on a crowded result page. In contrast, Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) structures your digital knowledge base so generative search algorithms (Google SGE, Perplexity) and voice agents immediately parse, extract, and synthesize your content as the singular authoritative answer.

When a prospect queries "How do I choose enterprise growth software?", answer engines do not present a list—they generate or speak a single answer. AEO ensures that answer quotes your brand.
