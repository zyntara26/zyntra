Got Questions About Enterprise AI?

Clear answers on hallucination prevention, telephony integration, and system latency.

What AI solutions does Zyntara provide? expand_more
Zyntara delivers four interconnected enterprise AI modules: (1) Custom conversational Website AI Chatbots trained on your proprietary documentation, (2) Ultra-low-latency AI Voice Agents handling inbound and outbound telephony, (3) Automated Lead Qualification algorithms that filter out unqualified inquiries, and (4) Autonomous CRM & Workflow automations connecting chat, WhatsApp, Twilio, HubSpot, Salesforce, and internal databases.
Can the AI chatbot book appointments directly onto our calendar? expand_more
Yes. Zyntara AI bots natively integrate with Calendly, Cal.com, Google Calendar, and Microsoft Outlook. Once an inbound prospect satisfies your qualification criteria (such as budget and authority), the agent presents live open slots directly within the conversation window, registers the booking, sends invite confirmations, and logs the deal in your CRM.
How realistic does the AI Voice Agent sound? expand_more
Our Voice Agent operates at a sub-400ms audio response latency, matching human speech cadences with zero noticeable lag. It incorporates emotional inflections, adaptive pacing, and natural handling of conversational interruptions. In live blind tests, over 91% of callers were unable to distinguish the AI voice from an experienced in-house customer service representative.
How does the system prevent AI hallucinations and false promises? expand_more
We employ a strict Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architecture coupled with deterministic business logic guardrails. The agent is explicitly prohibited from guessing. If a question falls outside your indexed documentation, the agent politely states it will verify with a human specialist and initiates an escalation protocol, guaranteeing zero reputational risk.
Which CRMs and sales software can Zyntara integrate with? expand_more
We provide turn-key two-way synchronization with HubSpot, Salesforce, GoHighLevel, Zoho, Pipedrive, Slack, Twilio, WhatsApp Business Cloud API, Make, Zapier, and custom REST API endpoints. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest using enterprise AES-256 protocols.
